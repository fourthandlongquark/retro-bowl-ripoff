How it plays

You are the quarterback and the head coach. Call a play from a 39-play book built on real football concepts (you get four that fit the down, and can swap for four more), snap it, find the open receiver, then run after the catch. On Easy and Normal your defense plays on its own, and how good it is depends on your roster. On Hard and Legend you play defense too: you control one defender at a time, chase the ball, and dive to make the tackle.

Throw: press anywhere on the screen (it does not have to be on the field), drag back like a slingshot, and release. On a phone, a second finger moves the QB while you aim. The ball goes the opposite way, and the further you pull, the further it flies. A dotted arc and a yard count show where it will land. You can also click or tap a receiver for a quick led pass. Keys 1–4 also throw to the numbered receiver. A green number is open, red is covered.

Interceptions: defenders can pick the ball off two ways. They can jump in front of a low throw while it is in the air, or beat your receiver to the spot where it lands. The yard count turns red and says DEFENDER when one is sitting where you are aiming. Throw over them with a longer pull, or pick someone else.

Run: WASD or arrows (on a phone, drag the left side of the field). F or Space dives for extra yards, or slides when you scramble with the QB. Shift jukes a tackler. Turn on Auto juke under the field and your runner jukes on his own, but it only fools the tackler about 6 times in 10, and after the first one or two in a play it almost always fails. A higher DOD rating gives him more good auto jukes per play (up to three). A juke you time yourself with Shift works more often, about 85 times in 100 for an average player and up to 95 with a high DOD rating, but it is not guaranteed either. Hit a receiver in stride, while he is running upfield, and he gets a burst of speed after the catch. Right after any catch the nearest defenders are a step slow, and your receiver will usually shake the first tackler on his own. Once the ball is caught the defense speeds up a little to chase.

Defense (Hard and Legend only): your player has a gold ring. WASD moves him, F or Space dives at the ball carrier, Shift (or a tap on the field) switches to the defender nearest the ball. Making the stop is mostly your job. Teammates cover, and sometimes make the tackle or knock a pass down themselves, but more often they only wrap the runner up and slow him until you get there to finish it.

After a touchdown: kick the extra point for 1, or go for 2 with a single play from the 2-yard line. The other team sometimes goes for 2 as well.

Celebrate: press G right after a touchdown, a first down, a big gain, a sack or an interception to show off. Press it again for a different move.

Career: 11 games, a four-team playoff, and the Pixel Bowl. Every player has a level from 1 to 100 built from ratings like speed, diving and dodging, and earns XP to raise them, you earn money to buy better players and a bigger fan base, and you draft rookies every offseason.
